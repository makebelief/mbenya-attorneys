Criminal law guide · GUIDE

Arrested in South Africa: First Questions to Ask

An arrest can be stressful. It helps to understand the allegations, the basis for police action and how to obtain professional assistance. Keep a record of relevant events, do not interfere with evidence and ask for advice tailored to the facts.

Applicable law and time limits depend on the facts and can change. Contact a qualified legal professional promptly if your rights or liberty may be affected.

When Can an Arrest Be Unlawful?

Not every arrest is unlawful, and outcomes turn on the specific circumstances. Important questions may concern the officer’s authority, the information relied upon and the manner of arrest. Time limits may apply to potential claims.
